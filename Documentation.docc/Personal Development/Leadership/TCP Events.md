# TCP Events

Ngee Ann Polytechnic's programme for high-performing students

## Overview

The Christiera Programme (TCP) is a programme for Ngee Ann Poly's high performing students, consisting of scholarship 
holders and the top 5% of students in each course.

@Small {
    It is named after a flower, and has no religious connections or function despite its name.
}

As a member of the Events subcommittee, I helped to plan the TCP Bonding Day and TCP Flagship.

## TCP Bonding Day

TCP Bonding Day is an event near the start of Semester 2 of each academic year, and is targeted 
towards new TCP members. 

It was our first event as the TCP Events Subcomm, and we were placed in a video call to get the
event done. I took lead along with some other (more outgoing than me) members, and we planned 
several games that teams would play in a round-robin format. One of the games was a "laser maze",
where we strung red tape between chairs and tables and had TCP members navigate through while 
holding hands.

## TCP Flagship

TCP Flagship is an event done in collaboration with Dialogue in the Dark and the Singapore 
Association for the Visually Impaired. We would run activities for the visually impaired clients,
allowing NP student volunteers the experience of working with people with a significantly different
world outlook than themselves.

I was assigned In Charge of Activity 1, which was a sachet making activity. I had to coordinate
a small team of Events subcomm members to plan out the materials to use and buy, along with run 
the event on the day itself.
