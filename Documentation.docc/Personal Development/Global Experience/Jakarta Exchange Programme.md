The Jakarta Exchange Programme was a collaboration between my school, SST, and BBS-PIK, a school in Jakarta, Indonesia

## Overview

The programme was designed to foster cultural exchange and collaboration between students from both schools.

The programme's primary focus was on developing and testing air quality sensors to determine the impact of air quality on student education.
However, the programme was primarily an immersion programme - SST students first visited BBS, learning about how they ran their lessons. We
also explored their city and learned about Indonesian culture.

Several months later, the BBS students visited SST in Singapore. SST has a newer learn-by-doing approach as opposed to BBS's more traditional
learn-by-watching approach, which was demonstrated using a math lesson where students would test each others' ability to differentiate equations.
