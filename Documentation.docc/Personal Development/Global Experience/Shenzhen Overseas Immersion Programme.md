# Shenzhen Overseas Immersion Programme

A collaboration with Shenzhen University and several Shenzhen tech companies

## Overview

This Overseas Immersion Programme (OIP) was organised by Ngee Ann Polytechnic's School of Engineering. We partnered
with Shenzhen University and explored some of their campus, including getting a look at their new Artificial Intelligence
labs. 

Outside of Shenzhen University, we also visited tech companies such as Mindray, a medical technology company. They 
demonstrated their automation-heavy approach to not only medical processes (e.g. automating medical testing), but also
with the manufacturing of the medical devices themselves.
