## Overview

I've always been interested in technology, but I am not been sure about which specific field I would like to go into.
I sign up for competitions to explore different fields of engineering, so they cover several different disciplines.

My competitions mainly fall under a few categories:
- Engineering and Product Development
- Robotics
- Software and App Development
- Competitive Programming
